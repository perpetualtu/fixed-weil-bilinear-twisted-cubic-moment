\begin{abstract}
We evaluate a smoothed bilinear twist of a shifted cubic product of the
Riemann zeta function.  For two independent divisor-bounded coefficient
sequences supported on $h\leq H$ and $k\leq K$, we prove the expected
three-term shifted main term, uniformly for shifts of size
$O((\log T)^{-1})$, with
error
\[
 O_{\eta,C,\eps}\!\left(T^\eps
 \{KH^{3/2}+T^{1/2}(HK)^{1/2}\}\right)
\]
whenever $H^{3/2}K\leq T^{1-\eta}$.  The proof fixes one Poisson orientation
throughout.  Its nonzero frequencies are controlled by finite completion and
the Weil bound after exact extraction of $(h,km)$.  The zero frequency is
evaluated with a coupled two-variable smoothing.  A full-kernel regulator and
genuine branchwise Tonelli domains justify the beta integrals; the diagonal
and zero-mode residues are assembled before analytic continuation or the
final regulator move.  A local three-divisor argument proves joint
holomorphy when the shifts collide.
\end{abstract}
